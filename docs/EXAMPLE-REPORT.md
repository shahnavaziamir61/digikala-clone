## Warnings (honest)

- Mainspring torque variation across working turns is high (linear model,
  no preload) — rate stability needs a flatter torque curve or fusee/constant-force device.
- All stresses are approximations — verify with FEA and prototyping.
- Material properties are typical values — verify with supplier datasheets.
- The 816 h figure assumes 1.86 µW consumption and 80% transmission efficiency;
  real friction and temperature will change it. **Measure, do not assume.**
